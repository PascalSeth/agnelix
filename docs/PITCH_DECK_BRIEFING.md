# Galien — Pitch Deck Briefing (Non-Technical)

> This document explains Galien in plain English for building a pitch deck. No code, no jargon — just what the product is, who it's for, why it matters, and how it makes money.

---

## 1. The One-Liner

**Galien is an AI sales assistant that finds local businesses, writes personal outreach emails to them, talks to them when they reply, and books meetings — automatically.**

Think of it as hiring a tireless junior salesperson who works 24/7: they search for prospects, research each one, write a custom email, follow up, answer replies, and put meetings on your calendar.

---

## 2. The Problem

Anyone who sells to local businesses (agencies selling marketing/web services to dentists, plumbers, salons, hotels, etc.) faces the same three painful steps:

1. **Finding prospects is slow and manual.** Hours spent scrolling Google Maps, buying stale contact lists, or guessing email addresses.
2. **Generic emails don't work.** Copy-paste templates get ignored or land in spam — people can spot a mass email instantly.
3. **Replies go cold.** When a prospect finally responds, the salesperson is busy with something else. By the time they reply, the lead has lost interest.

This is the daily grind of running outbound sales — and it's the #1 reason small agencies and consultants don't grow: **they spend more time prospecting than closing.**

---

## 3. The Solution

Galien replaces that entire manual process with one connected system:

| Step | What happens |
|------|--------------|
| **1. Find** | Tell Galien a type of business and a city (e.g. "dentists in Austin"). It searches Google Maps and pulls back real local businesses with contact details. |
| **2. Research** | Galien visits each business's website, checks how good (or bad) their online presence is, and builds a mini profile — what they do, what they're missing, who the decision-maker is. |
| **3. Write** | Using that research, Galien writes a personal email for each business — referencing real details about them, not a generic template. |
| **4. Send & Follow Up** | Emails go out from the user's own inbox (Gmail/Outlook), with automatic, timed follow-ups if there's no reply. |
| **5. Handle Replies** | When a prospect replies, Galien reads it, figures out what they mean (interested, objection, "not now", out of office), and either drafts a smart response or — if confident enough — sends it automatically. |
| **6. Book Meetings & Track Deals** | Galien can propose meeting times, add events to the calendar, and track every prospect through a simple pipeline (New → Contacted → Replied → Interested → Meeting Booked → Won/Lost). |

**The result:** the user shows up to a calendar full of meetings with businesses that already want to talk — without spending hours prospecting, writing, or chasing replies.

---

## 4. What Makes It Different

Most tools on the market only solve *one* piece of this puzzle — you'd normally need to stitch together a lead database, an email sequencer, a CRM, and a separate reporting tool (often 4-5 different subscriptions).

**Galien is all of that in one product:**

- **No "stack" required.** No need for separate tools like HubSpot (CRM), Instantly (email sequencer), or analytics dashboards — it's all built in.
- **Real personalization, not mail-merge.** Galien actually looks at each prospect's website and writes about *them* — not "Hi {{firstName}}" templates.
- **Closes the loop.** Most tools stop at "email sent." Galien continues through reply handling, objection responses, and meeting booking.
- **Human stays in control.** Before Galien sends anything automatically, there's a review window — so the business owner can step in if something looks wrong. It's "autopilot with a safety brake," not a black box.
- **Built-in sales coach.** An AI advisor inside the app ("Galien") gives strategy advice — like having a sales mentor on call.

## 5. Complete Feature Set (For Product/Demo Slides)

Beyond the core "find → write → send → reply → book" loop, Galien includes a full set of features that make it usable as a real agency operating system, not just an email tool:

### AI Personalization Engine
- **Website audits** — Galien scans each prospect's website (speed, mobile-friendliness, security, whether they have analytics/tracking set up) and turns the gaps into talking points.
- **Company research dossiers** — AI builds a short profile of each business (what they do, recent news, likely pain points) before writing anything.
- **Icebreakers** — AI generates a specific opening line per prospect, based on their actual website,business and reviews.
- **Decision-maker finder** — finds names, emails, and LinkedIn profiles of the right person to contact at each business.
- **Lead scoring** — AI ranks and tags leads (e.g. "high-value", "healthcare", "needs website help") so users focus on the best opportunities first.

### AI Sales Agent / Autopilot
- **Autonomous mode** — once trusted, Galien can research, write, send, and respond to replies on its own — with a short review window so the user can step in before anything risky goes out.
- **Reply understanding** — classifies every reply (interested, has a question, objection, "not now," out of office) and responds appropriately.
- **Battle cards** — the moment a prospect replies, Galien generates a one-page strategy sheet: what they likely care about, how to handle objections, and what to say next.
- **Proposal generation** — when a prospect asks for pricing/info, Galien can draft and send a proposal automatically.
- **Meeting booking** — proposes times and adds confirmed meetings to the calendar.
- **Nurture tracks** — leads who say "not now" aren't lost — they're automatically re-engaged on a long-term schedule (e.g. 30/60/90 days later).
- **Daily digest** — a summary email each day of what the AI agent did (emails sent, meetings booked, replies handled, anything flagged for review).
- **Goal-setting** — users set targets (e.g. "5 meetings/month", "8% reply rate") and Galien's behavior adapts to hit them.

### Industry "Playbooks"
- Pre-built outreach strategies for specific verticals (e.g. dental practices, trades, hospitality) — each playbook includes tailored email sequences, proposal templates, objection-handling scripts, and reporting templates.
- New users pick a playbook that matches their niche and get a ready-to-go strategy instead of starting from a blank page.

### Client Portals & White-Label Reporting
- For agencies running outreach **on behalf of their own clients**, Galien can generate a **branded client portal** — a shareable link where the agency's client can log in and see:
  - Campaign performance overview
  - AI-written progress reports (plain-English summaries of results, not just raw numbers)
  - Proposals and shared documents
  - A messages/communication thread
- Portals are branded with the agency's own logo and colors — the end client never sees "Galien," reinforcing the agency's own brand. This effectively replaces a separate client-reporting tool.

### Pipeline CRM & Reporting
- Visual kanban pipeline (drag-and-drop) from first contact to closed deal.
- Deal values and revenue tracking, attributed back to the campaign that produced them.
- Dashboard with key metrics: active campaigns, leads, emails sent, reply rate, meetings booked, revenue closed.
- Email open/click tracking on every message sent.

### AI Advisor ("Galien")
- A built-in chat assistant, available on every screen, that acts as an on-call B2B sales coach — answering questions like "how should I follow up with this lead?" or "what's a good angle for dental practices?" using the user's own business context.

## 6. Target Market

**Primary target market: small and independent digital marketing agencies that sell services to local businesses** (e.g. agencies offering web design, SEO, ads, or branding to dentists, contractors, salons, restaurants, hotels, and other local SMEs).

This is the single beachhead market to lead with in the pitch — it's the sharpest, most painful use case, and the easiest to sell into:

- **The pain is acute and constant.** New client acquisition is the #1 survival pressure for small agencies — most are 1-10 people with no dedicated sales hire, so the founder/owner does prospecting themselves, late at night, between client work.
- **They already buy tools for this.** These agencies are used to paying for SaaS (CRMs, lead lists, email tools) — so the buying behavior and budget already exist; Galien just consolidates and outperforms what they're stitching together.
- **The product was built around this exact persona.** Local-business lead discovery (Google Maps search), website-audit-based personalization, and white-label client portals are *not generic features* — they map directly to how a small agency operates and what it needs to look credible to its own clients.
- **Land-and-expand path is natural.** An agency that uses Galien to win its own clients can then resell Galien-powered outreach *as a service* to those same clients — turning each customer into a potential channel.

**Why narrow to this one segment for the pitch:** B2B service providers and solo consultants (mentioned elsewhere in this doc) are real secondary markets and natural expansion paths — but investors respond better to one sharply-defined wedge than three. Lead with "small agencies selling to local businesses," and frame the others as the expansion story (Slide: "Where we go next").

## 7. How It Makes Money

Galien is a **pure subscription (SaaS) business** — predictable, recurring monthly revenue with no dependency on commissions, ad spend, or revenue-share deals.

| Plan | Price | What you get |
|------|-------|---------------|
| **Starter** | $97/month | 500 lead credits, 1 user, email outreach, basic lead discovery |
| **Growth** | $297/month | 2,500 lead credits, 5 users, email + LinkedIn, A/B testing |
| **Agency** (most popular) | $997/month | 15,000 lead credits, unlimited users, AI reply handling, white-label, multi-client support |
| **Enterprise** | $2,997+/month | Unlimited everything, custom AI training, dedicated account manager |

**Why this model works — the case for investors:**

- **Recurring, predictable revenue.** Every customer pays monthly, every month, for as long as they're growing their business — classic SaaS economics (MRR, ARR, low revenue volatility).
- **Usage-based upside built in.** Credits cap how many leads/emails a plan covers each month. As a customer's outreach volume grows (more leads found, more campaigns run), they naturally outgrow their tier and upgrade — revenue expansion happens *without* a sales team pushing it.
- **High switching cost once adopted.** Once an agency's leads, sequences, sent-email history, pipeline, and (for Agency tier) their *own client portals* live inside Galien, ripping it out means re-platforming their entire sales operation and re-onboarding their clients to a new portal link. This drives strong retention.
- **Clear path to higher-tier upsell.** The product is intentionally tiered so growth is self-evident: more leads → more users → white-label/multi-client (Agency) → custom AI + dedicated support (Enterprise). The "Agency" tier specifically targets the primary target market (Section 6) at a price point ($997/mo) that's easily justified by landing even one new client.
- **Replaces a multi-tool budget.** The target customer is already paying for 3-5 separate tools (lead lists, email sequencer, CRM, reporting) often totaling $200-500+/month combined. Galien consolidates that spend into one subscription — making the price point an easy "like-for-like or cheaper" comparison, not a new cost.
- **Low marginal cost per customer.** The core costs (AI usage, email sending, hosting) scale with usage and are covered by the credit system — so gross margins improve as plans scale up.

## 8. The Customer Journey (for the deck's "how it works" slide)

A simple story arc to visualize:

1. **Sign up** with Google — takes under a minute.
2. **Quick setup** — tell Galien about your business, your tone of voice, and connect your email.
3. **"Find me 50 dental practices in Austin"** — Galien returns a list with contact info in seconds.
4. **Pick a few, hit "Launch"** — Galien researches each one and writes personalized emails.
5. **Emails go out** — tracked for opens and clicks.
6. **A reply comes in**: *"Sounds interesting, do you have time tomorrow?"*
7. **Galien drafts (or sends) a reply** and books the meeting on the calendar.
8. **Deal moves through the pipeline** — from first contact to "Won," with revenue tracked automatically.

This exact story is demonstrated in the product's interactive **Playground demo** — a guided walkthrough using a fictional dental practice, showing four real scenarios: a meeting getting booked, an objection being handled, a "not now" lead being nurtured long-term, and a full journey ending in a closed deal.
